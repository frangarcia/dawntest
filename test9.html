This page tests Exit Intent, On Click, On Scroll, Inactive Time and Total Time lightboxes.

If the destinations use Test Mode, add ?NGX_PM_TEST to this page's URL. Use &NGX_PM_TEST if other parameters already exist.

Section 1 — Lightbox trigger checks

  • Exit Intent: On desktop, move the pointer out through the top of the page.
  • On Click: Click the dedicated button at the top of this page.
  • On Scroll: Scroll the parent page past the destination's configured threshold.
  • Inactive Time: Stop interacting for the configured duration.
  • Total Time: Keep interacting while waiting for the configured duration.

Suggested settings: On Scroll at 50%, Inactive Time at 10 seconds and Total Time at 20 seconds. Configure these in the publishing destination editor.

Refresh between scenarios. Scroll inside the parent page, rather than inside an open lightbox, when testing On Scroll.
